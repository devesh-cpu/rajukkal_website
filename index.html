Our Vision

To unify and uplift the Kshatriya Raju community through education, employment, and mutual support.

We strive to create a structured platform that honors our ancestral roots while adapting to modern necessities, ensuring that every family within our community thrives together.

Historical Footprint

The Rajukkal community traces its origins back to noble Kshatriya lineages. Over centuries, our ancestors migrated and settled across prominent regions in Tamil Nadu, including Rajapalayam, Madurai, and Chennai, bringing with them a rich tapestry of culture, valor, and traditions.

Today, the Sangam acts as the custodian of this heritage, maintaining vital records, gothiram lineage data, and fostering unity among the diaspora.
